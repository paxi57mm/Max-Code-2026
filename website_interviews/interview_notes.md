# Website Interview Notes

I interviewed people about my website: what they think of my webpage, and what ideas they have for it. These are my notes from those conversations.

## Summary

One person pitched a badge-scan printer management system for print shops and fabrication businesses. You scan a badge and get a personal greeting like "Hello, Kevin, what would you like to print today?", and the request goes through a network server. Other people talked about a location-tracking app that shows how far away a person or "mark" is, and one person asked about grant money for school clubs. Nobody made any decisions or picked who would do what.

## Printer Management Idea

- **The main idea:** every shop that makes things with printers has trouble managing them, and the printer-management tools out there now aren't good enough. A badge-based system would replace them.
- **How it would work:** a worker at a print shop scans their badge at the machine, the screen greets them by name, and a network server handles the print request.
- **What's still missing:** we didn't talk about scope, hardware, budget, timeline, or next steps after the pitch.

## Other Ideas

- One person wants to build software that tells you how far away a person or "mark" is at any time.
- Another person asked if that would need a satellite, joking about asking NASA to follow one of its satellites.

## Funding Question

- Someone asked if a company gives grants to school clubs. Their example was a club that got $7,000 from one source.
- The answer: no one knew of a direct grant program, but if you can make a case and pitch it, there's a chance someone listening will decide to support it.

## Next Steps

- Nothing was agreed on.
- The only real request: someone asked for instructions on how to set up "the email system" once it's finished. No owner or deadline was set.
